🔇 I can't hear VoiceWord

  1. Check sound works in another app, like a video. If that's quiet too, the problem is your PC or headset, not VoiceWord.
  2. Using a Bluetooth headset? While any app uses its microphone (a call, dictating, or talking to another app), the headset switches to call mode, and normal sound can go quiet. Wait a few seconds after the call or dictation ends.
  3. Still quiet? Turn the headset off and on again. If it has a small USB dongle, unplug it, wait five seconds and plug it back in.
  4. Check the volume isn't turned down or muted, and that Windows is sending sound to the right place: Start, Settings, System, Sound, then look under Output.

✏️ My words didn't appear

  • Click where you want the words first, then hold your key and talk.
  • If you clicked somewhere else while VoiceWord was writing, a 📋 Paste here button appears on the VoiceWord bar. Click where you want the words, then press it.
  • You can also paste your last dictation any time: click the VoiceWord icon near the clock, then Paste last dictation.
  • A few windows (for example ones that ask for an administrator) don't let other apps type into them.

🎤 The microphone isn't working

  1. Open VoiceWord's Settings, then Microphone. Pick your microphone, then speak and watch the bar move. If the bar moves, VoiceWord can hear you.
  2. Check Windows lets apps use it: Start, Settings, Privacy & security, Microphone. Turn on Microphone access and Let desktop apps access your microphone.
  3. If you're in a call, the call may be using the microphone. Try again when it's finished.

🔔 The "microphone on" sound comes late

With a Bluetooth headset this is normal. The headset takes about a second to switch into call mode, and VoiceWord plays the sound once the microphone is really listening.

Start talking after the sound, and nothing you say is missed.

📄 Read page read the wrong part

  • Highlight a few words where you want it to start, then press Read page.
  • If VoiceWord can't find what you highlighted, it says so and reads from the top.
  • In Excel, select the cells you want to hear, then press Read page. To hear a whole row, click its row number first.

🛡 "Windows protected your PC"

You may see this the first time you install VoiceWord from our download page, because VoiceWord is new. Click More info, then Run anyway. The Microsoft Store version doesn't show this.

Using VoiceWord at work? Your IT team may need to allow it first. There's a page for IT teams you can send them.

🔑 The invitation code

  • The version from our download page asks for an invitation code once, the first time it opens. Paste the code Carly sent you, then press Start VoiceWord.
  • Capital letters, spaces and dashes don't matter.
  • Lost your code, or it says it doesn't work any more? Email voicewordapp@gmail.com and ask for a new one.
  • The Microsoft Store version never asks for a code.

💬 Still stuck?

  • Click the VoiceWord icon near the clock, then Help, then Send feedback or an idea. Tell us what happened.
  • Or email voicewordapp@gmail.com.
