VoiceWord privacy policy

Last updated 26 September 2026

In short: VoiceWord works on your PC. What you say, type and hear stays on your PC. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to send a problem report.

What stays on your PC

  • Your voice. When you dictate or use reading practice, your voice is turned into text on your PC. It is not saved as a recording and not sent anywhere.
  • Text you listen to. Reading aloud happens on your PC.
  • Writing checks and the AI helper. Spelling, grammar and the optional AI helper run on your PC.
  • The camera. Only used when you choose "Read a printed page". The picture is read on your PC and not kept or sent.
  • Your settings, word list, practice progress and dictation history. Kept on your PC only. You can turn history off, or delete it, in VoiceWord. Removing VoiceWord can remove all of it.
  • Files you save. If you use "Save as audio", the file goes where you choose to save it.

When VoiceWord uses the internet

  • Downloading its parts. The first time you set it up (and when you add features), VoiceWord downloads speech, voice, language and AI files from Hugging Face, GitHub and Ollama. These are downloads only; nothing about you is sent.
  • Checking for updates. The version from our own website checks GitHub for a newer version at most once a day. The Microsoft Store version gets updates from the Store instead.
  • Links you click. "Send feedback", "Support the app" (Ko-fi) and help links open in your web browser. What you do there is covered by those websites.

Problem reports – only if you choose

If VoiceWord closes unexpectedly, it may ask if you'd like to send a report. You can also send one from Help. Nothing is sent unless you click Send.

A report contains technical details only: the VoiceWord version, your Windows version, how much memory your PC has, whether it has an NVIDIA graphics card, the error details, and VoiceWord's own log with personal details removed. If you type a note, your note is included.

A report never contains anything you said, typed or listened to.

Reports go privately to the developer through Google Forms (Google). They are only used to fix problems in VoiceWord, and are deleted once they're no longer needed.

Asking for an invitation

While VoiceWord is being tested with a small group, you can ask for an invitation on our website. That form asks for your name and email, and an optional note. They go privately to the developer through Google Forms (Google), and are only used to send your invitation and reply to you. They are never shared or sold, and you can ask for them to be deleted at any time.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising.
  • No tracking or analytics.
  • We never sell or share your information.

Children

The VoiceWord app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

VoiceWord is made in New Zealand and follows the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020. If you've sent a problem report or an invitation request and want it deleted, or have any question about privacy, email voicewordapp@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
