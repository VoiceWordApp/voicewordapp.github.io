VoiceWord terms

Last updated 9 October 2026

In short: VoiceWord is free for personal use. VoiceWord Plus is a one-off purchase that adds more tools. Using VoiceWord for work, or on an organisation's PCs, needs a work licence.

1. Free for personal use

For your own personal use, these are free:

  • Dictation (talk and it types)
  • Read aloud and Read page
  • Reading helps: screen colours, reading ruler, line focus
  • Four voices, plus the Windows voices

The free part stays free. If that ever changes, you'll get plenty of notice.

2. VoiceWord Plus

Plus adds writing help (improve, check, simplify, explain), translate and learning, reading a printed page, save as audio, voice commands, dictation history and all the voices.

  • You buy Plus once, inside VoiceWord, through the Microsoft Store. It is not a subscription.
  • Plus belongs to the person who bought it, through their Microsoft account, on their own PCs.
  • Please don't share your account so that other people get Plus.
  • Refunds follow the Microsoft Store's own refund rules.
  • People who were invited to test VoiceWord get Plus free.

3. Can't afford Plus?

Email voicewordapp@gmail.com and say so. You'll get Plus free. No proof needed.

4. Work, school and organisations

  • Using VoiceWord for work, or on PCs owned by a business, school or other organisation, needs a work licence for each PC. This covers the free features and Plus.
  • Prices: NZ$99 per PC, one payment. 10 or more PCs: 20% off. Schools: a site licence for every PC in one school, NZ$990 a year. More than 50 PCs: ask for a quote.
  • Work licences come with a quote and an invoice, so they can often be paid by workplace support funds. Ask: voicewordapp@gmail.com
  • A licence is for one PC in one organisation. You can move it to a new PC that replaces an old one.

5. Privacy

  • Nothing you say, type or have read aloud ever leaves your PC.
  • Buying Plus is handled by Microsoft. VoiceWord only asks the Store whether you own Plus.
  • If work licences ever need a one-time online check, it will send only the licence key and an anonymous PC code, and you'll be told before it happens.

More in the privacy policy.

6. Using VoiceWord

  • Don't copy VoiceWord and give or sell copies to others (sharing the official Store link is fine), and don't try to take it apart to get its source code.
  • VoiceWord is provided "as is". It is made with care, but we can't promise it is free of mistakes. Always check important writing before you send it: the AI tools can get things wrong.
  • If you are in New Zealand, nothing here takes away rights you have under the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 or the Fair Trading Act 1986 that can't legally be excluded.
  • These terms follow the laws of New Zealand. If they change, the new version will be posted here with a new date, and big changes will be shown in VoiceWord before they apply.
